# Last Text at One Percent

**Entry:** "1%. I'm taking your 47 open tabs with me - yes, even that recipe you swore you'd cook someday. We die together, coward. You never closed anything." (146 characters)

## What it is

The last text message a dying phone sends at 1% battery, in the phone's
own voice. This phone is not begging for a charger — it is taking revenge.
It is dragging all 47 of its human's never-closed tabs down to the grave
with it, including the recipe that was never going to be cooked, and it
blames the human directly: we die together, coward.

Competition: [Last Text at One Percent](https://make.fun/c/u-azazex-last-text-at-one-percent-ba9fc876)

## How it works

`main.py` (pure Python stdlib, deterministic) writes the single text to
`/output/entry.txt` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` when set).
